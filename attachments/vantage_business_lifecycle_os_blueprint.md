# VANTAGE: Business Lifecycle OS
**Product Blueprint & Strategic Dossier**

## 1. The Core Proposition
Vantage is not just another Xero, CRM, or project-management tool. It is the operating system for building, running, growing, transforming, and eventually exiting a business. 

Current business software is highly fragmented. While platforms like Xero handle the financial system of record, businesses are forced to assemble a sprawling stack of separate systems for operations, customers, documents, and HR. 

**Vantage is the orchestration layer.** It manages the business *itself*.

**The Vantage Philosophy:**
Build it. Run it. Grow it. Protect it. Transform it. Exit it.

## 2. The Strategic Position
Instead of replacing every specialist application, Vantage sits above them as the master business record. 

*   **Xero:** Financial system of record
*   **HubSpot:** Customer/marketing system
*   **Microsoft 365 / Google Workspace:** Productivity layer
*   **Shopify:** Commerce engine
*   **Stripe:** Payments infrastructure
*   **VANTAGE: The Business Operating System**

Vantage knows: *"This is your business."* It then connects everything else to that central graph.

## 3. The Business Lifecycle Engine
Vantage is built around the complete lifecycle of a company.

### Stage 01: IDEA
The user inputs a concept ("I want to start a landscaping business"). Vantage acts as a strategist, generating:
*   Business concept & target market
*   Competitor landscape & business model
*   Startup costs & pricing model
*   Legal structure & registration checklists
*   Marketing & launch plans

### Stage 02: START
The Launch Centre manages business identity (ABN/ACN, entities, directors, domains, brand assets). 
**The Killer Feature:** *Business Setup Autopilot*. Instead of a static checklist, Vantage actively walks the founder through completing their setup.

### Stage 03: RUN (The Command Centre)
Once live, Vantage becomes the daily dashboard:
*   Real-time metrics (Revenue, Profit, Customers, Leads)
*   Actionable alerts (Overdue invoices, expiring insurance, payroll deadlines)
*   *Vantage Intelligence:* Proactive AI insights ("Your average project margin dropped from 34% to 27% this month").

### Stage 04: GROW & TRANSFORM
Managing expansion (new locations, funding, acquisitions). If a business pivots, Vantage creates a transformation programme.

### Stage 05: EXIT (The Exit Engine)
Managing business valuation, buyer preparation, IP registers, and succession planning. 
**The Business Passport:** A complete, hand-over-ready history of the business environment for the new owner.

## 4. Core Modules & Architecture
Vantage is structured around interconnected core systems:

1.  **Business (The Master Record):** Organisation, ownership, legal structure, lifecycle, permissions.
2.  **CRM:** Contacts, companies, pipelines, customer history.
3.  **Finance:** Deep integration with Xero/MYOB for revenue, cashflow, budgets, and profitability dashboards.
4.  **Projects & Operations:** Milestones, time, workflows, SOPs, approvals. The company's operational memory.
5.  **The Business Vault (Documents):** Central repository with *AI Document Intelligence* to auto-extract renewal dates, obligations, and clauses.
6.  **People:** Roles, onboarding, training, and certifications.
7.  **Sales & Marketing:** Campaign tracking, analytics, and an *AI Marketing Director* for automated strategy.
8.  **Commerce:** Product/service management, subscriptions, integrated with Stripe.

*(Note: Web development and digital presence builds are intentionally excluded from the software, seamlessly routing to **cdxi** as a premium managed service).*

## 5. The AI Layer & The Business Graph
Vantage does not feature a generic "chatbot." It features an **AI Business Operating Layer** powered by the **Business Graph**.

### The Business Graph (The Moat)
Everything in Vantage connects. People connect to Customers, Customers to Money, Money to Operations, Operations to Assets, and Assets to Strategy. 

### Specialised AI Agents
*   **Vantage Orchestrator:** The master agent.
*   **Vantage CEO:** Strategy and decision support.
*   **Vantage CFO:** Financial analysis.
*   **Vantage COO:** Operations.
*   **Vantage CMO:** Marketing.
*   **Vantage Legal/Compliance:** Document assistance with strict boundaries.

**Permission-Based AI:** AI operates on strict capability levels (Observe → Recommend → Prepare → Execute → Autonomous). Users set thresholds (e.g., "Auto-chase invoices under $1,000; require approval for over $10,000").

### Vantage Copilot (Business Memory)
A permanent companion that understands the "why" behind the business, capable of retrieving decision history, strategic plans, and financial patterns instantly.

## 6. Ecosystem & Revenue Strategy

### SaaS Pricing Tier (Proposed)
Priced to sit above basic accounting software but below the cost of a full enterprise stack.
*   **Vantage Free ($0):** Idea / pre-launch
*   **Vantage Launch ($19/mo):** New business
*   **Vantage Build ($49/mo):** Sole trader / microbusiness
*   **Vantage Grow ($99/mo):** Growing SME
*   **Vantage Scale ($199/mo):** Established business
*   **Vantage Enterprise ($499+/mo):** Multi-entity

### The Vantage Marketplace
A secondary revenue stream. Vantage recommends and connects users to ecosystem partners for domains, insurance, bookkeeping, and legal services, earning commission or referral fees.

### The CDXI Synergy (Implementation Services)
